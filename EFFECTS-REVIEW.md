# Effects review (synth4, BST build)

> **Historical (superseded in v96).** This review describes the synth4 / BST build. Since v96 (build 996) the tracked synths (ANALOG, ARCADE, INSTRUMENT, ENSEMBLE, CHOIR), CROSS OSC and SQUARE SYNTH HQ are retired, and POLY SHIFTER runs on the filter bank rather than a phase vocoder. For current behaviour see [SOUNDS-AND-CONTROLS.md](SOUNDS-AND-CONTROLS.md), [POLY-SHIFTER.md](POLY-SHIFTER.md) and [WHATS-NEW-v96.md](WHATS-NEW-v96.md).

What was checked, what was changed, what is left. The numbers come from `tools/research/level_audit.c` (every live type, one block on at a time, the five bundled guitar loops at the pedal's level, BS.1770 loudness against the same loop with the block off). They are reproducible: `python3 tools/research/level_trim.py` recalibrates `engine/px_trim.h`.

## 2. Loudness

Measured with every knob at noon (the boosts' level controls at unity), against the guitar alone. The old trim table came from a chain that had amps and cabinets, and the SYNTH block had no trims at all. Now every trimmed type is within 0.0 dB of the guitar at noon (mean over the five loops), by construction; the BST boosts are not trimmed but set at their own unity (FIXED WAH and MAXIMIZER about +1 dB over the guitar on purpose, RANGEMASTER within 0.3 dB below); the FIXED WAH's make-up grid is hand-calibrated (engine/px_pedals.h, tests/pedal_levels.mjs + pedal_levels_measure.py), not generated by level_trim.py. "Before" is with the previous trims.

* The big offenders were the synths: ARCADE +13.6 dB, INSTRUMENT +16.6, ENSEMBLE +16.7, CHOIR +9.1, ANALOG +8.9, POLY OCT FUZZ +5.9, SQUARE SYNTH HQ +6.8, ENVELOPE WAH +8.2. SMALL STONE was -3.1.
* A synth's trim scales **the synth only**: ARCADE's guitar share (MIX) is no longer trimmed with it (`engine/px_dsp.c`, the mono synth branch).
* The boosts keep their design: MAXIMIZER is about +1.4 dB and FIXED WAH +1.0 dB at their unity setting, RANGEMASTER is 0 at VOLUME 50, EP BOOST 0 at GAIN 0, ENHANCE 0 at LOUDER 0.
* Limits: this is integrated loudness over five loops, so a loop that sustains differently moves a type by a few dB either way (SLOW SWELL spans -2.8 to +8.8 dB across the loops, LO-FI RADIO -5 to +4). Levels at other knob positions follow the knobs (compressor, octave, synth LEVEL and EQ gains swing 8 to 30 dB between 20 and 80). The trims level the types at noon, not the knobs.
* The synth voices are now 6 to 17 dB quieter than before at the same knob positions. If a pad should sit above the guitar, raise its LEVEL, or change the target in `level_trim.py`. The unit tests of the synth voices read the voices without the trim.

## 5. Efficiency

Host cost per second of audio (relative only; the pedal's own figures are in docs/PEDALS.md and the readings from `tools/research/pedal_load.mjs`):

* Heavy: POLY OCT FUZZ 3.2 ms/s (the ERB bank; about 70 % of a core on the pedal), RANGEMASTER 2.2 (a 2x2 Newton solve per sample), CHOIR 2.0, ENSEMBLE 1.9, POLY OCTAVE HQ 1.9, EP BOOST 1.5, LOWEST OCTAVE HQ 1.4, INSTRUMENT 1.4, ANALOG 1.3, SQUARE SYNTH HQ 1.2, ARCADE 1.2.
* Everything else is 0.2 to 0.8.
* RANGEMASTER's core already uses the cheap forms (a polynomial exp, a linear predictor for the Newton start, an early exit at 2e-5 V, a clamped step), so there is no easy win without measuring on the pedal. EP BOOST, MAXIMIZER, FIXED WAH (one biquad, coefficients only recomputed while the knob moves) and ENHANCE are cheap.
* FIXED WAH's Q (researched after the first review): it was a fit with no source and far too sharp (the peaking biquad's Q is not the -3 dB Q; at 18 dB the real Q was about 17). It now follows measurements of Vox-style wahs (music-electronics-forum thread "a Vox wah project", post 83): -3 dB Q 2.4..3.3 at the toe and 8.5..11.7 at the heel (Q about 1/frequency), converted exactly to the biquad's Q; the make-up grid was recalibrated. ElectroSmash gives no Q. Still not measured on a V847 itself.
* **Solver tolerances loosened (done, measured).** RANGEMASTER's Newton solve averaged 2.0-2.4 iterations per sample against a floor of 1 (the predictor already starts it close); the stopping step was 2e-5 V. At 1 mV the output stays within -93 dB of the tight solve (below 16-bit noise) and the iterations drop to 1.5-1.9: RANGEMASTER 2.2 -> 1.9 ms/s (-14 %). EP BOOST's JFET solve stopped at 1e-9 A; at 1e-6 A (0.1 % of the bias current) it takes 1.0-1.5 iterations instead of 2.0-2.3, within -99 dB: 1.5 -> 1.2 ms/s (-20 %). Levels did not move. (The iteration counts and the error against the tight solve were measured on the chords, riff and lead loops with a counting copy of the solver; not kept as a tool.)
* **Per-effect research round.** Done for the pieces that cost or decide the most; everything else was checked against the repo's own research notes (docs/research/*):
  * Transistor / JFET pedals (RANGEMASTER, EP BOOST): a per-sample Newton-Raphson solve is the standard method (Yeh, "Automated physical modeling of nonlinear audio circuits", TASLP 2010, the DK method; the Queen's University Belfast work on Newton robustness uses this very pedal); look-up-table K-methods trade memory for iterations, and a forum report of a Big Muff emulation quotes 2.6 iterations per sample, so 1.5-1.9 here with a linear predictor is already at the efficient end. A 2-D table replacement would change the sound and needs listening on the pedal: not done.
  * Poly octave (POLY OCTAVE HQ, LOWEST OCTAVE HQ, POLY OCT FUZZ): the Aalto thesis "Real-time polyphonic octave doubling for the guitar" compared candidate methods and found ERB-PS2 (doubling the phase of constant-ERB IIR sub-bands) best overall; that is what the ERB bank is. The code already has the cheap forms (11.025 kHz bank behind two half-band stages, a fast inverse root instead of a division, voices skipped while their level is zero, the bank idling when silent). No change.
  * Ensemble / chorus (ENSEMBLE, CHOIR, the MOD chorus): three modulated delay taps with offset LFOs and a bandwidth-limiting low-pass is the usual Solina-style structure (Dattorro's chorus notes; BBD papers at DAFx); all-pass or Lagrange fractional reads beat linear for the colouring. Nothing here is cheaper without losing the character.
  * FIXED WAH: see section 1 and PEDALS.md (the Q law).
  * Still open: the same literature round for every remaining type (delays, reverbs, modulation, EQ, compressors) was not repeated type by type; those are 0.2-0.8 ms/s each and none stands out. The pedal's own CPU readings (`tools/research/pedal_load.mjs`) are the next measurement.

## 7. Added after the review (v75, same branch)

* **HUM FILTER** (EQ block): notches at the mains and its harmonics, MAINS 50 HZ / AUTO / 60 HZ (a detector listens for which hum is in the signal and follows a change in about a second), DEPTH 3..40 dB, WIDTH 1..20 Hz, HARMONICS 1..5. A true notch with its own width and depth (a peaking cut's skirts took 3 dB off an A at 110 Hz). `tests/engine/hum_test.c`.
* **Staged load shedding** (`firmware/px_shed.h`): 4 blocks over 85 % -> the heaviest block is faded out (not the gate), up to 3, then the old BYPASS. Screen: SHED. Editor: PING flag bit 2, command 30 SHED (protocol 8), hatched tiles, "Bring back", and a "may not fit" warning from `docs/pxcost.js` (host timings scaled by one pedal reading: an estimate). `tests/engine/shed_test.c`, `tests/shedsim.c`, `tests/test_editor.mjs`.
* **POLY SHIFTER** (PITCH block): a peak-locked phase vocoder (Laroche and Dolson 1999), 1024-point frame, 17 ms latency, chords and any interval, [POLY-SHIFTER.md](POLY-SHIFTER.md). Its limits are stated there (partials closer than about 170 Hz blur; level moves up to 2 dB with the interval). About 29 KB of pool RAM (72 KB left), about 28 % of an audio block by estimate, **26 % of an audio block on a BlackBox (973), peaking at 61 %**. `tests/engine/pv_test.c`.
* **Tuner** (`firmware/px_tuner.h`, `tests/tunersim.c`): reads 25 Hz to 1.5 kHz (was 61 Hz to 1.1 kHz), a proper decimator, early-exit YIN, a phase-advance refinement of the fundamental (YIN alone read synthetic inharmonic strings about 2 cents sharp), smoothing and in-tune hysteresis, a reference A of 415 to 466 Hz on the knob. On synthetic strings within 1.5 cents from 31 Hz to 1.3 kHz. Not heard on a real guitar.
* **Global settings** (`px_global_t`, `firmware/px_store.h` st_cfg, editor protocol 9): input high-pass, 5-band output EQ, output limiter, TAILS (a delay or reverb plays out when switched off or left by a preset change), smart gate HUSH, tuner reference and lights. `tests/engine/global_test.c`, `tails_test.c`, `hush_test.c`, `tests/test_store.c`.
* **Footswitch gestures** (double tap in STOMP mode, hold in both): per preset, an action each (block, BYPASS, tuner, next / previous preset, STOMP / PATCH). `tests/uisim.c`. **Tuner lights**: needle and strobe on the seven button LEDs (`tests/lightsim.c`).
* **Stage view** in the web editor (`docs/edstage.js`).

## 9. New factory bank and no retired-type message (v75)

* **No more "RETIRED TYPE"**: `px_preset_canonical` turns a retired type (the 27 old SYNTH types, SFX APPLAUSE, the SFX pedals that moved to BST) into the first live type of its block, switched off, its settings kept; it runs when a slot is read from the flash, when the editor sends a preset and for a factory slot. The pedal's screen and the editor have no such message (`ui_type_label`, `docs/edsound.js`); the engine still bypasses a tombstone it is handed directly (`tests/test_audio.mjs`).
* **A new 52-preset factory bank** (`engine/px_dsp.c`, FACT: the groups are in docs/SOUNDS-AND-CONTROLS.md): clean and boost, pitch, synths, modulation / delay / reverb, special effects, INIT. Each preset's PRESET LEVEL is set by `tools/research/preset_levels.py` (the mean loudness on the five guitar loops within 0.15 dB of the dry guitar); `tests/engine/factory_test.c` keeps names, types, loudness and peaks honest, and `tests/test_editor.mjs` the processor share (worst, POLY FUZZ, 53 % by the estimate).
* `px_preset_base` is the blank starting point the engine tests use now (they used factory slot 7 for it).
