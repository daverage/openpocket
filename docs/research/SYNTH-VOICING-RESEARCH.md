# Brass and strings voicing references

## Brass

- **Mellotron-style guitar brass.** EHX describes the MEL9 BRASS voice as trumpet-like on single notes and a section on chords. Its attack control sets the rate of a brightness sweep, and another control adds subtle lip buzz at note onset. The dry and effect levels are independent. The manual describes the sound and controls, leaving the internal signal processing unspecified. [EHX MEL9 manual](https://www.ehx.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/mel9-manual.pdf)
- **Analog synth brass.** Roland's JP-8080 example uses a sawtooth oscillator with separate pitch, filter, and amplifier envelopes. Its Jupiter-8 brass stab layers slightly detuned saws, gives one oscillator a small upward pitch gesture at onset, and opens a low-pass filter with the amplitude envelope. The evolving tone and attack give this patch its character. [Roland JP-8080 manual](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/JP-8080_OM.pdf) · [Roland Jupiter-8 brass stab with sound example](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/#brass-stab)
- **Acoustic brass.** Fletcher and Tarnopolsky measured a quiet trumpet spectral envelope with a broad maximum near 1 kHz, plus upper harmonics that extend as playing level rises. The exact response varies by note, instrument, and playing level. STK's brass waveguide exposes a different architecture with pitch-dependent bore delay and lip resonance and a blowing-pressure envelope per voice. [Trumpet measurements](https://www.phys.unsw.edu.au/music/people/publications/Fletcheretal1999.pdf) · [STK Brass source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thestk/stk/master/src/Brass.cpp)

**OpenPocket direction.** Treat the current input-derived BRASS as a chord-playing analog voice. Bring wet loudness back into line with dry guitar. Start with a broad midrange body and add the upper-mid bite as the attack opens, with a gentle level-dependent drive. Softly played notes should stay darker than accents. Heavy saturation of an entire guitar chord can introduce sum and difference tones. An acoustic trumpet or brass-section voice would benefit more from tracked per-note oscillators, waveguides, or licensed samples than from another static EQ change.

## Strings

- **Divide-down string ensemble.** The original Solina service information shows the master oscillator, dividers, key gates, sustain, formant circuitry, and modulator in its signal flow. The ensemble stage adds movement with modulated delay paths. The strings already have shaped, sustained excitation before that stage. [Eminent Solina service information](https://manuals.fdiskc.com/flat/ARP%20Solina%20Schematics.pdf) · [Arturia Solina V manual and downloads](https://www.arturia.com/support/downloads-manuals/product/solina-v)
- **Analog string patch.** Roland's Jupiter-8 tutorial combines a narrow pulse with a slightly detuned second oscillator, a swell with full sustain and a longer release, high- and low-pass filtering, and ensemble chorus. Its examples provide a useful reference for a vintage string-machine pad. [Roland Jupiter-8 analog strings with sound example](https://articles.roland.com/sound-design-jupiter-8-model-expansion-poly-presets/#analog-strings)
- **Guitar string pedals.** EHX sells analog-string, tape, PCM, and orchestral flavors as different sounds. Its SYNTH9 String Synth uses tone and attack controls. STRING9 Symphonic places its octave-down contribution on the bottom guitar strings, while its PCM voice offers vibrato depth, and the Freeze programs hold notes and chords. MEL9 describes its small-section strings as having natural vibrato. [EHX SYNTH9 manual](https://www.ehx.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/synth9-manual.pdf) · [EHX STRING9 manual](https://www.ehx.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/string9-user-manual.pdf) · [EHX MEL9 manual](https://www.ehx.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/mel9-manual.pdf)
- **Physical bowed strings.** The STK Bowed model supplies continuing bow excitation through its bow table and delay lines, plus body filtering. It has dedicated bow velocity, position, and pressure controls. These provide a basis for a tracked solo bowed voice as a separate project. [STK Bowed source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thestk/stk/master/src/Bowed.cpp)

**OpenPocket direction.** Treat STRINGS as a vintage string machine. Suppress the pick transient with a bowed swell, make the wet path brighter and more reed-like before the ensemble, and keep the sub octave modest or limited to the bottom register. Listen for a stable center with gentle ensemble movement rather than making the shared octave-up signal the whole identity. A guitar-derived swell still follows the string's decay. For an orchestral or tape alternative, investigate separate voices with note-wise excitation or spectral sustain.

## Listening checks

Render the same clean DI through the dry, old, and revised modes. Compare versions at matched wet RMS before judging timbre. Also check their unnormalized levels at identical settings, then sweep DRY independently. Use quiet single notes, accented stabs, close dyads, open chords, and held notes. For BRASS, listen for a round onset that brightens and clear articulation without a sudden level jump. For STRINGS, listen for a reduced pick transient, steady sustained pitch, and ensemble motion without strong octave flutter. [Bundled DI loops](../../assets/loops/ATTRIBUTION.md)
